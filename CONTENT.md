# Content notice

Kindred is a general-purpose chat app. Everything runs on your own computer with models you choose and download yourself.

- **18+ content is off by default.** It can only be switched on in Options after confirming you are an adult (18+). It can be switched off again at any time.
- **No explicit imagery.** Even with 18+ content enabled, image generation keeps its safeguards: characters are always depicted as adults and the app does not produce explicit images.
- **Scene photos follow the selfie rules.** Photos taken during a Together scene use the same safeguards as selfies: adults only and nothing explicit, with revealing worn gifts only shown in private places and always framed tastefully.
- **Characters are adults, except protected family.** Every character, including the bundled samples, is an adult. The only exception is a protected family character under 18 (e.g. your son, daughter or younger sibling), which is allowed only with a family relationship. They are never pictured (no selfies, avatar generation or people in photos), never involved in romance or 18+ content even with 18+ enabled, and can only be friends, rivals, acquaintances or family with other characters. Imported cards that describe a minor in a sexual or romantic way are refused. Family-style relationships (e.g. grandparent, sibling) are always non-romantic.
- **Weather is optional and the only online call.** See "Network use" in the README.
- The AI models you use are third-party software and can produce inaccurate or unexpected text. You are responsible for the models you install and how you use them, and for following their licences and local laws.
